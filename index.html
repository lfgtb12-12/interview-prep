How review scheduling works / Add more cards later

Local review status lives in this browser via localStorage. “Solid” schedules a review in 3 days on first success, then 7 and 14 days on subsequent successes; “Fuzzy” schedules tomorrow; “Drill again” schedules today. The 7 cards are built into the HTML, and new cards can be added to the embedded data array in a future version. You can also ask ChatGPT to extend this deck with more interview problems.

Updated and browser-tested · Made for active learning: the front stays short, the back explains why, and the histogram includes a step-through wall-finding animation.
